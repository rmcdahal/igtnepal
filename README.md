## 🛡️ Overview & Engineering Principles

Conventional gas regulators sold in South Asia are passive pressure-reducers that continue dispensing gas even when rubber hoses rupture or slip off burner nipples. This flaw is the leading cause of residential and commercial kitchen flash-fires and cylinder explosions in Nepal.

The **Danish IGT Regulator** is engineered in Denmark by **Integrated Gas Technologies (IGT)** compliant with stringent European standard **EN 16129** and international gas safety certifications (**CE 0085, DVGW, ISO 9001**).

Designed specifically for South Asian LPG mixtures (typically **60% Butane / 40% Propane** in Nepal), the Danish IGT device integrates active safety valves, automatic shut-off triggers, and real-time hydrostatic pressure manometers.

## 🛠️ 5-Step Installation & Leak-Testing Protocol

```
[LPG Cylinder] ──(25.6mm Clip-On)──> [Danish IGT Device] ──(Brass Nipple)──> [Braided Hose] ──> [Gas Stove]
                                             │
                                     [Push-Button Test]
                                             │
                              [Manometer Pressure Stable? ]
                                     ├── YES ──> Safe to Ignite
                                     └── NO  ──> Auto Shut-off (Leak Detected)
```

1. **Step 1: Inspect Cylinder Collar**: Ensure the cylinder's rubber O-ring seal is seated properly inside the valve neck. Wipe off any grit or dust.
2. **Step 2: Mount the Regulator**: Lift the black collar ring, press the regulator firmly downward onto the cylinder neck until a clean metallic "click" sounds, and release the ring.
3. **Step 3: Turn the Switch Clockwise**: Turn the operating lever 90 degrees clockwise to the "ON" (vertical) position.
4. **Step 4: Hydrostatic Leak Test**:
   - Turn OFF all stove burners.
   - Gently press the **Auto-Cut Reset Push-Button** on the regulator once.
   - Observe the manometer needle rise into the **Green (Pressure OK)** zone.
   - Wait 15 to 30 seconds.
   - If the needle remains stationary: **Your entire kitchen gas line is 100% airtight and leak-free**.
   - If the needle drops toward zero: **A micro-leak exists in your pipe, burner valve, or clamp**. The device automatically locks flow.
5. **Step 5: Safe Ignition**: Once the test passes, you may ignite your gas stove with total peace of mind.

## 🔍 Anti-Counterfeit Verification

Because of the high reputation of Danish IGT devices, unauthorized cheap copies frequently circulate in local hardware markets. Always verify authentic markings before installation:

1. **Laser-Engraved Serial Number**: Genuine Danish units have crisp, clean computer-engraved serial numbers etched into the metal casting, never painted or printed on stickers.
2. **Manufacture Date Stamp**: Stamped MM/YY code (e.g. `06/26` for June 2026) indicating factory test batch.
3. **IGT Denmark Logo Casting**: The official IGT logo is cast into the zinc casing alongside European standard markings `EN 16129` and `CE 0085`.
4. **QR Code & Hologram**: Official retail packaging contains a verifiable QR security sticker linked to [https://igtnepal.com/genuine-igt](https://igtnepal.com/genuine-igt).

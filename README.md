## 🛡️ What is the Danish IGT Gas Regulator?

The **Danish IGT Gas Regulator** is a specialized LPG safety device designed and engineered in Denmark by **Integrated Gas Technologies (IGT ApS)**. Unlike passive pressure reducers, the IGT gas regulator is an active kitchen safety management system certified under European standard **EN 16129** and international safety marks (**CE 0085, DVGW, ISO 9001**).

Specially adapted for the Nepalese market and regional LPG compositions (commercial & domestic butane-propane mixes), the **IGT gas regulator** incorporates dual automatic shut-off triggers and a calibrated hydrostatic manometer pressure gauge.

## ⚡ Key Safety Features of the IGT Gas Regulator

### 1. Excess Flow Auto Cut-Off
The core mechanism inside the **IGT gas regulator** is an internal precision-engineered brass shut-off valve. When flow rate exceeds the safety threshold of 1.5 kg/h (such as when a pipe tears, burns, or slips off), the valve instantly snaps shut, stopping LPG flow at the cylinder neck.
- Explore: **[How the IGT Auto Cut Gas Regulator Works](https://igtnepal.com/igt-auto-cut-regulator-nepal)**

### 2. Built-In Manometer Gauge (Leak Detection & Gas Level)
The **IGT gas regulator with meter** includes a sealed hydrostatic pressure gauge that performs two vital functions:
- **Instant Leak Detection**: Press the reset button with stove knobs off; if the needle drops within 15–30 seconds, a micro-leak exists along your hose or stove.
- **Gas Level Monitoring**: Displays remaining LPG pressure with a color-coded dial (Blue = Full, Green = Adequate, Red/Yellow = Refill Soon).
- Explore: **[IGT Gas Regulator with Meter in Nepal](https://igtnepal.com/igt-regulator-with-meter-nepal)**

### 3. Reinforced 25.6mm NS 533 Compact Collar
Built strictly to Nepal Standards (NS 533 / NOC specifications) using multi-ball ball-bearing snap locking, ensuring zero-play airtight coupling with all Nepali LPG gas cylinders.

## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Installation & Leak-Testing Guide

```
[LPG Cylinder] ──(25.6mm NS 533)──> [Danish IGT Gas Regulator] ──(Brass Nipple)──> [Steel-Braided Hose] ──> [Stove]
                                                 │
                                         [Reset Push Button]
                                                 │
                                  [Manometer Pressure Holding? ]
                                         ├── YES ──> Airtight (Safe to Ignite)
                                         └── NO  ──> Auto Shut-Off (Leak Detected)
```

1. **Step 1: Inspect Cylinder Collar**: Check the rubber O-ring seal inside your LPG cylinder collar for cracks, dirt, or displacement.
2. **Step 2: Mount the IGT Gas Regulator**: Lift the black collar ring, press the regulator firmly downward onto the cylinder neck until a clean metallic "click" sounds, and release the ring.
3. **Step 3: Rotate the Lever ON**: Turn the switch 90 degrees clockwise into the vertical (ON) position to pressurize the line.
4. **Step 4: Hydrostatic Leak Test**:
   - Turn OFF all stove burners.
   - Firmly press the **Auto-Cut Reset Push-Button** on the IGT gas regulator once.
   - Verify that the manometer needle rises into the **Green (Pressure OK)** zone.
   - Wait 15 to 30 seconds.
   - If the needle remains completely steady: **Your gas line is 100% leak-free and secure**.
   - If the needle drops toward zero: **A micro-leak is present in the hose, burner, or clamp**. The device prevents gas release.
5. **Step 5: Safe Kitchen Ignition**: Once the test confirms zero leaks, light your stove with confidence.

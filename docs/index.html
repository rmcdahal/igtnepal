Safety Protocol

How to Install the Danish IGT Gas Regulator

Follow certified European operating procedures to install and test your Danish IGT gas regulator.

1

Collar Inspection

Inspect the cylinder valve rubber O-ring seal for cracks, dirt, or displacement prior to mounting.

2

Snap Connection

Lift the locking ring, press the IGT gas regulator firmly downward until a sharp metallic click locks the collar.

3

Switch to ON

Rotate the lever 90 degrees clockwise into the vertical ON position to pressurize the line.

4

Hydrostatic Test

Press the reset push-button. Ensure the gauge needle stabilizes in the Green zone for 15-30 seconds.

5

Safe Ignition

Ignite your gas stove. If pipe leaks develop at any point, the IGT gas regulator seals off supply automatically.

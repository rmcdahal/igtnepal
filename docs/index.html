Safety Protocol

5-Step Installation & Leak-Testing Procedure

Follow certified European standard operating guidelines for connecting your Danish IGT regulator.

1

Collar Inspection

Inspect the cylinder valve rubber O-ring seal for cracks, dust, or deformity prior to mounting.

2

Snap Connection

Lift the locking ring, firmly press downward onto the cylinder until a sharp metallic click locks the collar.

3

Switch to ON

Rotate the lever 90 degrees clockwise into the vertical ON position to pressurize the line.

4

Hydrostatic Test

Press the reset push-button. Ensure the gauge needle stabilizes in the Green zone for 15-30 seconds.

5

Safe Ignition

Ignite your gas stove. If pipe leaks develop at any point, the device seals off the supply automatically.
